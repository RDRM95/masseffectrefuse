    ABOUT THIS PRODUCTION

    Mass Effect: Refuse is an unofficial, non-commercial fan film: an animated alternate ending to Mass Effect 3 in which Shepard rejects the Catalyst's three choices and the galaxy — prepared since Sovereign's attack in 2183 — defeats the Reapers through conventional war. Every ship, environment, character silhouette, line of dialogue and bar of music here is original work created for this project. No game assets were extracted or redistributed.
