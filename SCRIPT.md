# MASS EFFECT: REFUSE — The Ending We Earned
## Screenplay (as produced)

All dialogue is original. Timings are seconds from the start of each chapter and match the subtitle cues in `src/compositions/*.tsx`.

---

### PROLOGUE — THE CYCLE (1:18)

BLACK. Music: a low drone, a slow synthetic arpeggio.

> For millions of years, the civilisations of the Milky Way had risen and fallen.
>
> Each believed itself the master of its own destiny.
>
> Each encountered the same ancient enemy.
>
> The Reapers.

Silence. A deep hit.

> In 2186, the machines returned.
>
> But this time, the galaxy was ready.

EARTH FROM ORBIT. An Alliance dreadnought crosses close to camera. Another follows, then a cruiser. The Home Fleet is revealed in echelon over the terminator.

TITLE: MASS EFFECT / REFUSE / THE ENDING WE EARNED.

In the far distance, points of red light appear. Cut to black.

### CHAPTER ONE — THE PREPARED GALAXY (1:24)

EARTH ORBIT. Defence platforms, the Home Fleet. Reapers descend. Tactical interface: *Sol Defence Network — Home Fleet*.

**HACKETT (radio, 10.5):** All ships, this is Hackett. Hold formation. Let them come to us.
**HACKETT (14.8):** Platforms, you have weapons release on my mark.

A Sovereign-class advances and fires. Cruiser *Shasta* is destroyed.

**ALLIANCE CONTROL (20.2):** Cruiser Shasta is gone. Lead capital is still coming.
**HACKETT (23.0):** Second Division, concentrated fire. Same section. Make it count.

Dreadnought volleys. Barrier impacts bloom on the Reaper. Platform Four's reticle closes.

**ALLIANCE CONTROL (29.0):** Impacts on target. Barriers degrading. Platform Four has a solution.
**HACKETT (34.0):** Platform Four. Mark.

The hyper-mass accelerator fires. The projectile crosses space. The Reaper's hull fractures and the capital ship breaks apart.

**HACKETT (43.0):** Good kill. Reform on the platforms. This is not over.

PALAVEN. The Hierarchy's line. Reaper destroyers and a capital ship attack. A Turian cruiser is lost.

**TURIAN COMMAND (54.0):** All groups, hold the line. Nothing reaches the surface.
**TURIAN COMMAND (60.0):** Concentrate on the lead capital. Every gun.

Combined fire destroys the destroyer, then the capital ship.

**TURIAN COMMAND (67.5):** Target destroyed. Hold position. Rotate the damaged groups out.

> Earth held.
>
> Palaven held.

### CHAPTER TWO — THE CATALYST (1:52)

THE CITADEL, Wards open, the Crucible docked. Earth burns below.

THE CHAMBER. Shepard, limping, walks the last metres to the dais. The Catalyst appears: a figure of light.

**CATALYST (15.5):** You have reached the Crucible. Few cycles ever do.
**SHEPARD (19.5):** Then I won't waste it. What are you?
**CATALYST (22.0):** The intelligence that directs the Reapers. I preserve order between organic and synthetic life.
**CATALYST (26.0, red light rises on the right):** You may destroy them. Every synthetic dies with them, including the ones you call crew.
**CATALYST (30.0, blue light rises on the left):** You may control them. Your mind replaces mine. You would not remain yourself.
**CATALYST (34.5, a green column at the centre):** Or synthesis. Organic and synthetic made one, whether they consent or not.
**CATALYST (40.5):** The conflict will continue. Your resistance cannot change that.
**SHEPARD (44.0):** We've already changed it.
**CATALYST (47.0):** Your forces have suffered substantial losses.
**SHEPARD (50.0):** So have yours.
**CATALYST (53.0):** Your military advantage is temporary.
**SHEPARD (56.0):** That's what you keep telling yourself.
**SHEPARD (60.0):** We know how to fight you now. We've got the fleets, the shipyards, the resources.
**SHEPARD (65.0):** And we're not fighting alone.
**CATALYST (68.5):** The alternatives I have presented would end the conflict.
**SHEPARD (72.0):** On your terms.
**SHEPARD (75.0):** You've been deciding our future for millions of years.
**SHEPARD (78.5):** That ends now.
**CATALYST (81.0):** You choose refusal.
**SHEPARD (83.0):** I choose us.

Silence. The figure of light fades. The three paths go dark.

> The Crucible did not fire.

EXTRACTION. The Normandy at the Citadel.

**HACKETT (radio, 90.3):** Normandy, this is Hackett. The Crucible is dark. Get our people out of there.
**JOKER (93.3):** Already on approach. EDI's got two life signs at the beam site.
**EDI (95.8):** Commander Shepard and Admiral Anderson. Both alive.

FLAG BRIDGE, SSV ORIZABA — six hours later. Hackett and Shepard across the strategy table.

**HACKETT (100.0):** The Crucible?
**SHEPARD (101.6):** Not firing.
**HACKETT (103.2):** Understood.
**SHEPARD (105.6):** We can still win this.
**HACKETT (107.6):** I know.
**HACKETT (109.6):** Let's get back to work.

### CHAPTER THREE — THE LONG WAR (1:06)

> The refusal was not the victory.

ARCTURUS SHIPYARDS, month three: a dreadnought under construction. MENAE DOCKS: the Hierarchy Fourth Fleet deploys. SUR'KESH: Special Tasks Group analysis.

**SALARIAN INTEL (17.0):** Barrier emitters cycle every eleven seconds. Hit the same plate inside that window and it stays down.

JOINT OPERATIONS: asari, Alliance, geth and quarian ships kill a Reaper destroyer together.

**FLEET (24.0):** All groups, synchronise on the asari lead. Fire on her mark.

KROGAN GROUND FORCES land on an occupied world. CERBERUS BLACK FLEET raids a staging area.

**CERBERUS (35.0):** Black Fleet, weapons free. Hit the staging area and fade before they can turn.

NORMANDY, reconnaissance at a relay.

**EDI (41.0):** Fourteen capital signatures at the relay. They are holding position, Commander. They are not advancing.

THE STRATEGIC MAP. Red contracts; blue expands; months pass.

**HACKETT (50.0):** They're not replacing their losses. We are. Keep the pressure on every front.
**SHEPARD (56.0):** Hold the corridors open. Nobody fights alone out there.

---

### CHAPTER FOUR — THE REAPERS RESPOND (0:36)

INSIDE THE NETWORK. Red data over an immense hull.

> HARVEST PROJECTION — REVISED · ORGANIC INDUSTRIAL OUTPUT: EXCEEDS MODEL BY 340% · HOMEWORLDS NEUTRALISED: 0 OF 7 · CAPITAL UNITS LOST: 74 — NOT REPLACEABLE

**HARBINGER (8.0):** This cycle deviates from every projection.
**HARBINGER (12.0):** Their homeworlds stand. Their yards outproduce our losses. They have learned where we break.
**HARBINGER (17.0):** Dispersal invites destruction in detail. We will not disperse.
**HARBINGER (21.0):** All formations will converge. I will assume direct control.
**HARBINGER (26.0):** One harvest. One field. Nothing will be held back.

A wall of Reapers moves toward the camera.

---

### CHAPTER FIVE — THE FINAL HARVEST (1:24)

THE ALLIED GRAND FLEET assembles at the Hades Nexus approaches.

**HACKETT (4.5):** All fleets, this is Hackett. Everything we have is here. Execute the plan.
**VICTUS (10.0):** Hierarchy fleets in position. We hold the right. Spirits watch over all of us.

THE BATTLE. Harbinger's beam destroys cruiser *Kyoto*.

**FLEET (17.0):** Harbinger's firing. Cruiser Kyoto is gone.
**HACKETT (20.0):** Keep the line. Don't give it a clean shot at the dreadnoughts.
**VICTUS (27.0):** Indomitable is hit. She's still firing.
**EDI (33.0):** Escort formation is collapsing. Harbinger is drawing clear of the group.
**SHEPARD (38.0):** That's the opening. Everything on Harbinger. Same section, on the count.

THE FIRING PLAN. Synchronised volleys and Thanix lances strip the barrier from section seven.

**FLEET (49.0):** Barriers at sixty percent. Solution holding.
**HACKETT (55.0):** Thanix batteries, sustain. Platform Kilimanjaro, you have the solution.
**FLEET (60.0):** Barrier failure confirmed. Kilimanjaro, you are clear.

The hyper-mass accelerator fires. The round strikes. Harbinger's limbs tear away; internal explosions; its weapons go dark. For a moment it is motionless. Then the hull collapses.

Silence but for a low drone.

**FLEET (75.0):** Harbinger is down.
**HACKETT (79.0):** Confirmed. All groups, remaining targets. Finish it.

---

### CHAPTER SIX — VICTORY (0:42)

HADES NEXUS: the last Reaper capital ship is destroyed.

**FLEET (9.0):** Target destroyed. Flag, that was the last one on the board.

CONFIRMATION across every network: Alliance, Hierarchy, geth consensus, STG, asari, quarian, Cerberus. *Operational Reaper formations: none. Active Reaper command structure: none.*

**FLEET (16.0):** All networks confirm. There's nothing left out there.

FLAG BRIDGE. Hackett alone.

**HACKETT (20.0):** All stations, this is Admiral Hackett.
**HACKETT (24.0):** Reaper command capability has been eliminated.
**HACKETT (27.0):** The remaining hostile formations have been destroyed.
**HACKETT (32.0):** The war is over.

THE CIC. Crews receiving the announcement. Relief. Disbelief.

**HACKETT (38.4):** There are still people out there.
**HACKETT (40.3):** Begin recovery operations.

### CHAPTER SEVEN — THE FUTURE (1:24)

EARTH, reconstruction. PALAVEN. THESSIA. TUCHANKA at sunrise, Wrex and Eve on a ridge above the new city.

**WREX (20.5):** Took the galaxy long enough to work it out. The krogan were worth keeping.

RANNOCH: geth and quarian ships side by side. THE CITADEL. CERBERUS: the Illusive Man, seated, Earth beyond the window.

**ILLUSIVE MAN (36.5):** Humanity sits at the head of the table now. Whatever they say about how we got here, they'll say it from behind our ships.

NORMANDY OBSERVATION DECK. Joker, EDI, Tali, Shepard, Garrus, Liara.

**GARRUS (43.0):** So. No beam, no last-second miracle. Just a lot of very large guns.
**SHEPARD (47.0):** You sound disappointed.
**GARRUS (49.0):** I calibrated half of them. I'm taking the credit.
**TALI (52.3):** Keelah. We're alive. We're actually alive.
**LIARA (55.4):** I keep waiting for someone to give the next order.
**SHEPARD (58.4):** No more orders tonight.
**JOKER (60.4):** So... shore leave?
**EDI (61.9):** I believe that is the appropriate response, Jeff.

FINAL SHOT. Shepard at a viewport. Alliance ships pass; civilian traffic returns; the yards are working. The camera withdraws until Earth fills the frame.

> THE CYCLE WAS BROKEN.
>
> THE FUTURE WAS NOT GIVEN.
>
> IT WAS WON.

MASS EFFECT: REFUSE — THE ENDING WE EARNED. END.
